  evidence tiers.** Dominikowski et al., *Frontiers in Endocrinology* (2026-06-18),
  https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2026.1822475. Contrasts peer-reviewed PK/PD and

  review that substantiates the Task 3 proxy set for the GH-axis peptides

  reaffirms that AOD-9604 has no established human efficacy on this panel. No

## (d) Regulatory changes — **action item for clinic customers**

**FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) — meeting July 23–24, 2026.**
The official FDA meeting page lists the following bulk drug substances for
**503A Bulks List** consideration, four of which are tracked peptides:

| Peptide (in our 20) | Nominated substance forms | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **BPC-157** | BPC-157 (free base) / BPC-157 acetate | On PCAC agenda for 503A review |
| **KPV** | KPV (free base) / KPV acetate | On PCAC agenda for 503A review |
| **TB-500** | TB-500 (free base) / TB-500 acetate | On PCAC agenda for 503A review |
| **MOTS-c** | MOTS-c (free base) / MOTS-c acetate | On PCAC agenda for 503A review |

- **Docket:** FDA-2025-N-6895 (regulations.gov). **Written-comment window
  reported as open through July 9, 2026.** Nominators will present in support of
  inclusion at the meeting.
- **Why it matters to clinics:** none of these four is FDA-approved or carries a
  USP/NF monograph. Their legal availability for **503A compounding** hinges on
  whether PCAC/FDA place them on the Bulks List. A decision *not* to list keeps
  them outside lawful compounding; a decision *to* list would create a defined
  compounding pathway. Either outcome changes what clinic customers may compound,
  prescribe, or market for these four peptides.
- **Context (secondary sourcing, lower confidence):** trade/legal commentary
  reports that in **April 2026** the FDA removed BPC-157 and TB-500 from 503A
  **Category 2** without moving them to Category 1, leaving a regulatory gray
  zone pending this PCAC review. Treat the April action as **unverified against a
  primary FDA document** in this run — confirm directly before relying on it.

No FDA **recall** or MAUDE adverse-event signal was retrievable this run (MAUDE
tool subscription-gated). One UK-relevant human adverse-effect signal appears in
the literature (Melanotan depigmentation, 10.1093/ced/llag221) but no MHRA alert
could be confirmed (MHRA tool subscription-gated).

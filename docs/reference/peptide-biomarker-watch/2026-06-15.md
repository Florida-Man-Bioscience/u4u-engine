## (c) New human evidence

1. **Thymosin Alpha-1 — anti-tumor immune remodeling (CBC-differential signal).**
   *PRaG 5.0* (Kong et al., 2026), a single-arm Phase II in 43 heavily
   pretreated advanced refractory solid-tumor patients, integrated personalized
   Tα-1 (baseline-T-lymphocyte-stratified dosing; 1.6 mg thrice weekly
   maintenance) into a PD-1-inhibitor + radiotherapy + GM-CSF regimen. Flow
   cytometry showed significant increases in CD8⁺ T cells, NK cells, and CD4⁺
   effector-memory T cells, with a significant decrease in Tregs (p = 0.001).
   No Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs. ORR 30.8%. NCT05790447.
   (10.1200/jco.2026.44.16_suppl.2516)

2. **Thymalfasin (Tα-1) — peripheral CD8⁺ expansion, neoadjuvant gastric cancer.**
   Liu et al. (2026), prospective Phase II (n=30), anti-PD-1 (serplulimab) + SOX
   + 9 weeks thymalfasin before gastrectomy. Flow cytometry: expansion of CD8⁺
   T cells with increased CD69; PBMC RNA-seq showed upregulation of
   antigen-processing/presentation, type I IFN, and innate-sensing programs.
   pCR 30.0%, MPR 56.7%. NCT06461910.
   (10.1200/jco.2026.44.16_suppl.4103)

   *Interpretation:* Both directly support the matrix's only human-tier cell
   (Tα-1 → CBC differential, lymphocyte/CD4:CD8 ↑) and the Task-3 proxy
   "absolute lymphocyte count ↑ · CD4:CD8 ↑." Both are oncology-adjunct contexts
   (combined with PD-1 blockade and radio/chemo), so the lymphocyte signal is
   confounded by co-interventions — supportive, not isolating, evidence.

3. **Kisspeptin — LH/FSH/testosterone dynamics under continuous infusion.**
   Naveed et al. (2026), *Hormones*. Three healthy men received continuous IV
   kisspeptin-10 (12.5 mcg/kg/h, 24 h). LH rose from 2.8–3.8 to 17.5–30.6
   mIU/mL (5–8× baseline) within 12–20 h, then declined 13–47% from peak
   (partial desensitization of the non-pulsatile receptor response); FSH and
   testosterone rose modestly in parallel. NCT01438073. No editorial notices.
   (10.1007/s42000-026-00795-y)

   *Interpretation:* Confirms the existing kisspeptin proxy (LH↑/FSH↑ primary,
   sex steroids↑). Adds a tracking-relevant nuance: with continuous (vs. bolus)
   administration the LH proxy is biphasic and partly self-limiting — relevant
   if the engine models kisspeptin response kinetics.

## (d) Regulatory changes — **action item for clinic customers**

**Headline: 12 of the 20 tracked peptides are in an active FDA 503A compounding
review, and 4 more are in APA litigation — 16 of 20 in a federal process.** This
is the single most consequential regulatory item for Peptodyssey's clinic
customers and it materially expands last week's 4-peptide entry.

**1. PCAC meeting July 23–24, 2026** (Federal Register notice **2026-07361**,
published Apr 16, 2026; FDA advisory-committee calendar). Seven tracked peptides
nominated for the **503A Bulk Drug Substances List**, each as **free base and
acetate**:

| Meeting day | Peptides (in our 20) | Proposed use (per nomination summaries) |
|---|---|---|
| **Jul 23** | **BPC-157**, **KPV**, **TB-500**, **MOTS-c** | BPC-157 → **ulcerative colitis** (strongest dossier: early human GI history under code PL-14736); KPV → anti-inflammatory; TB-500 → tissue recovery; MOTS-c → metabolic |
| **Jul 24** | **Emideltide (DSIP)**, **Semax**, **Epitalon** | sleep/insomnia; cognition / cerebral ischemia; longevity. Docket-wide indications also include opioid withdrawal |

**2. Second PCAC meeting before end of February 2027** — five more tracked
peptides for the 503A Bulks List: **GHK-Cu** (skin/hair), **Melanotan II**
(pigment/libido; carries the clearest safety flag — melanoma risk), **LL-37 /
cathelicidin** (antimicrobial), **Dihexa** (cognition), **PEG-MGF** (pegylated
mechano growth factor; muscle).

**3. April 2026 Category 2 removal — now corroborated (confidence upgraded).**
FDA removed these 12 peptides from 503A **Category 2** (the "significant safety
concerns / do-not-compound" bucket they were placed in during **September 2023**)
within seven calendar days of updating its 503A bulk-substance categories,
teeing them up for the two PCAC reviews. **Removal from Category 2 is not
approval and is not placement on Category 1** — these peptides remain unapproved
/ investigational and were never lawfully compoundable under 503A. Last week this
action was flagged *unverified*; it is now corroborated via the Federal Register
notice (2026-07361) and Hyman Phelps FDA Law Blog analysis (citing FDA's 503A
categories document).

**4. Litigation track (separate from PCAC).** *Evexias Medical Centers /
Farmakeio v. FDA* (Texas, Administrative Procedure Act) concerns four more
tracked peptides — **AOD-9604, CJC-1295, Ipamorelin acetate, Thymosin Alpha-1** —
challenging their Category 2 placement. Combined with the PCAC 12, **16 of the 20
tracked peptides are in an active federal regulatory or legal process.**

**5. Not named in either track (of our 20):** **GHRP-2, Kisspeptin, Selank** —
status unchanged this week.

**6. Process caveats to convey to clinics.** PCAC recommendations are
**non-binding**; even a "yes" vote still requires **notice-and-comment
rulemaking** (typically 12–18 months) *unless* FDA issues **enforcement
discretion** (the mechanism used for compounded GLP-1s during shortages), which
could open practical access within weeks of a favorable vote. Written-comment
dockets are open ahead of the meeting (**docket FDA-2025-N-6895** for BPC-157,
with parallel dockets for the others); **reported comment deadlines differ across
sources (July 9 vs July 22, 2026)** — verify current status on regulations.gov
before advising customers. **503B outsourcing facilities:** FDA has **not** moved
these peptides to the 503B Category 1 list, so clinic supply routed through 503B
remains outside a defined lawful pathway.

**7. GLP-1 backdrop (not in the 20, but the compounding signal clinics will ask
about).** FDA proposed to **exclude** semaglutide, tirzepatide, and liraglutide
from the **503B bulks list** (Federal Register **2026-08552**, May 1, 2026; FDA
press announcement), with the comment period closing **June 30, 2026**; discussed
in Hyman Phelps "Riders on the Storm Part 3" (Jun 26, 2026) re: FY2027
appropriations riders. Direction of travel: **tightening** on GLP-1 compounding
even as the 12 wellness peptides move toward *possible* access.

No FDA **recall** or MAUDE adverse-event signal was retrievable this run (MAUDE
subscription-gated). No MHRA alert could be confirmed (MHRA subscription-gated).

CRITICAL REGULATORY GRAY ZONE — Action Required Before Compounding

BPC-157 and TB-500 were removed from Category 2 (effective ~April 22–23, 2026) after nominators withdrew safety nominations. They are NOT in Category 1 and are NOT on the 503A Bulks List. This creates a legal gray zone: compounding is neither explicitly authorized nor explicitly prohibited under current FDA policy. PCAC review is scheduled for July 23–24, 2026. Consult legal counsel before compounding any peptide. Comment deadline for PCAC review: July 9, 2026 via regulations.gov.

Medspa Clinical Risk Assessment

High Risk — Do Not Compound

Category 2 Peptides

Ibutamoren, Ipamorelin, GHRP-2, Kisspeptin-10 remain in Category 2. Compounding or administering these exposes the medspa and prescriber to active federal enforcement action under 503A/503B.

Moderate Risk — Legal Gray Zone

Removed from Cat. 2, Not in Cat. 1

BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, Semax, Epitalon, MOTS-C, and others are in a gray zone. Compounding is neither explicitly authorized nor prohibited. Consult legal counsel; enforce documentation; await July PCAC outcome.

Lower Risk — Currently Legal

Category 1 Compoundable

GHK-Cu (non-injectable, topical) is in Category 1 and legally compoundable under 503A with valid prescription. Ensure pharmacy uses registered API manufacturer with valid CoA.

Compliance — Advertising Rules

Marketing Restrictions Persist

Even when peptides move to Category 1, compounding pharmacies and medspa providers may NOT make claims of safety or efficacy implying FDA approval. All promotional materials must be reviewed regardless of category change.

Supply Chain — Quality Standards

API Sourcing Requirements

All 503A compounding requires bulk drug substances from FDA-registered manufacturers with valid Certificates of Analysis. Pharmaceutical-grade, GMP-compliant API supply chains are a prerequisite for any future legal peptide compounding.

Timeline Reality Check

Final Rulemaking is 1+ Years Away

PCAC July 2026 recommendations do not equal legal authorization. Final notice-and-comment rulemaking to add peptides to the 503A Bulks List is expected to take over a year after PCAC votes. Gray market remains high-risk throughout.
